Frequently Asked Questions & ATS Resume Tips

Learn how MaxerZ helps job seekers pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), format resumes for human recruiters, and generate tailored AI cover letters.

What is an ATS Friendly Resume?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly resume uses clear, standard headings, parseable PDF formatting, zero tables or complex graphics, and includes targeted keywords matching the job description. MaxerZ formats all PDF exports to pass 100% of modern ATS parsers.

How does the AI Cover Letter Writer work?

MaxerZ analyzes your recipient details, job role, and company description, cascading across premier AI language models to compose a highly persuasive, customized cover letter that connects your work experience to the target position.

How does the ATS Score Reviewer work?

Our 7-stage ATS engine evaluates your resume against the target job description across 6 dimensions: Content Relevance, ATS Parseability, Layout Formatting, Visual Consistency, Grammar & Language, and Completeness. It provides a 0–100 score and action-item rewrite suggestions.

Can I merge multiple PDF files online?

Yes! The MaxerZ PDF Merger tool lets you upload up to 5 PDF files (resumes, cover letters, portfolios, certifications), reorder their sequence with drag-and-drop ease, and merge them into a single consolidated PDF document.
